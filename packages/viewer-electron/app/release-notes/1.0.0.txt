Viewer 1.0, the first release.

- Browse, preview and search your files in a spatial workspace of planes.
- Previews for more than 300 formats: documents, Office files, camera RAW, 3D models, CAD drawings, email, certificates, archives and more. Any file can be shown as hex.
- Lay out photos and PDFs as collages, contact sheets, N-up sheets, booklets, cards, labels or posters, saved as PDF, PNG or JPEG.
- Nest DXF parts for laser and plasma cutting, with the sheets, cut length, time and cost it takes.
- Copies on the same disk are instant. Viewer never deletes your files permanently: removals go to the Trash.
- Local agents and models can help explore a folder, always with your approval.
- Check for Updates is in the Viewer menu.

Requires macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
